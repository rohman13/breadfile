Extract selected pages from a PDF

Pull out only the pages you need and save them as separate PDF files in one convenient ZIP package.

What you can do

  • Choose pages from a visual preview
  • Save each selected page as a PDF
  • Keep the source document on your device

Three simple steps

  1. Open your PDF.
  2. Mark the pages you want to extract.
  3. Download the selected pages in a ZIP file.

Extract PDF Pages questions

What format are extracted pages saved in?

Each selected page is saved as its own PDF and packaged in a ZIP download.

Will the original PDF be changed?

No. BreadFile creates new files and leaves your original document untouched.

Your documents stay private.

BreadFile processes files locally in your browser. Your PDF is not uploaded to our servers.
