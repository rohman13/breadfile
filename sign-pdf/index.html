Sign a PDF privately in your browser

Draw a signature, create a handwritten or script mark, or upload a stamp. Place and resize it precisely before downloading the signed PDF.

What you can do

  • Draw, type, or upload a signature
  • Move and resize marks on any page
  • Keep signatures and documents on your device

Three simple steps

  1. Choose a PDF and create or upload your mark.
  2. Place, move, and resize it on the document.
  3. Download the PDF with the visible signature added.

Sign PDF questions

Is this a cryptographic digital signature?

No. BreadFile adds a visible electronic signature or stamp, like signing printed paper. It does not issue a certificate-based digital signature.

Can I sign more than one page?

Yes. Navigate between pages and place one or more marks wherever they are needed.

Your documents stay private.

BreadFile processes files locally in your browser. Your PDF is not uploaded to our servers.
