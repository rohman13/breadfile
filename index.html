Free private PDF tools

Useful PDF chores, without uploads

BreadFile is a free collection of private PDF tools for everyday document work, with no uploads required. You can merge PDF files, split a PDF into smaller documents, compress image-heavy pages, add a visible signature, rotate sideways pages, organize page order, and convert between PDFs and images. Each tool runs directly in your browser, so your documents do not need to be uploaded to BreadFile.

Choose a task below to open its dedicated workbench. There is no account to create and no software to install. When the job is finished, BreadFile downloads a new file while leaving your original document unchanged.

Merge PDFCombine several PDF files in your preferred order. Split PDFSeparate pages by ranges, intervals, or visual selection. Extract PDF pagesSave selected pages as individual PDF files. Delete PDF pagesRemove blank or unwanted pages from a document. Rotate PDFTurn one, several, or every page the right way up. Organize PDF pagesReorder, duplicate, rotate, or remove pages visually. Compress PDFReduce the size of image-heavy PDF documents. Sign PDFDraw, type, upload, place, and resize a visible signature. Images to PDFCombine JPG, PNG, or WebP images into one PDF. PDF to imagesExport every PDF page as JPG or PNG.

How private browser processing works

Traditional online converters send a document to a remote server for processing. BreadFile instead uses browser technologies such as PDF-LIB and PDF.js on your device. That local approach avoids file-transfer waiting and keeps document contents out of our servers. Very large files may still depend on the memory available on your phone, tablet, or computer.

BreadFile is built with appreciation for the open-source work of BentoPDF, whose privacy-first PDF toolkit helped inspire this project.

BreadFile is best suited to routine PDF chores: joining reports, pulling out a page, correcting orientation, preparing images, or adding a visible signature. For certificate-based digital signatures, archival compliance, or legally regulated workflows, use a specialist service that supports those requirements.
