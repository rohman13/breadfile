Split a PDF into the files you need

Cut one PDF into smaller documents by ranges, intervals, or selected pages. Everything is processed locally in your browser.

What you can do

  • Split by custom page ranges
  • Create a file every set number of pages
  • Select pages visually before downloading

Three simple steps

  1. Choose the PDF you want to split.
  2. Select ranges, intervals, or pages.
  3. Cut and download the resulting PDF or ZIP.

Split PDF questions

Can I split only certain pages?

Yes. Use visual selection or enter page ranges to choose exactly which pages belong together.

Does splitting reduce PDF quality?

No. BreadFile copies the selected PDF pages without rasterizing them.

Your documents stay private.

BreadFile processes files locally in your browser. Your PDF is not uploaded to our servers.
