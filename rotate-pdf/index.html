What you can do

  • Rotate individual PDF pages
  • Turn every page left or right at once

Three simple steps

  1. Open the PDF.
  2. Rotate individual pages or use the batch controls.
  3. Download the corrected document.

Rotate PDF questions

Can I rotate only one page?

Yes. Each page has its own rotation controls, and there are also controls for the entire document.

Is the rotation permanent?

The original file is unchanged. The downloaded copy includes the rotations you selected.

Your documents stay private.

BreadFile processes files locally in your browser. Your PDF is not uploaded to our servers.
