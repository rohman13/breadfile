Compress PDF questions

Will compression affect quality?

Compression can reduce image detail. Choose a lighter setting when visual quality matters most.

Why does a text-only PDF shrink very little?

Text and vector content is already compact. This tool has the largest effect on PDFs containing large images.

Your documents stay private.
